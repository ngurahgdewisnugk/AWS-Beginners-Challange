## AWS Free Tier

When you sign up to AWS, you automatically get access to 17 enable region with available services :   
1. Compute Services
2. Machine Learning Services 
3. Containers Services
4. Storage Services
5. Analytics Services 
6. Database Services
7. Migration & Transfer Services
8. Secuirty, Identity & Compliance Services 
9. Networking & Content Delivery 
and etc

The AWS Free Tier includes three different types of free offers
1. Receive up to $200 USD in credits
2. Includes free usage of select services
3. No charges incurred unless switch to the Paid Plan

After my 6-month or 200 $ Credits Free Tier period ends,
* My Account Will Be Terminated
* My account will automatically upgrade to a paid plan if you commence use of AWS Organizations, or certain other AWS services and offers.
